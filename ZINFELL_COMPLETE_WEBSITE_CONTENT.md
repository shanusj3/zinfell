- **Page Title (SEO):** `Planning Your First SaaS MVP: Scope, Architecture & Execution | Zinfell Insights`

#### Key Insights & Frameworks
- Define the Single Core Value Loop: Identify the one critical action that solves 80% of the user's primary pain point.
- Resist Premature Microservices: Build a clean, modular monolith first. It allows rapid refactoring without distributed latency.
- Standardize Auth & Billing Early: Don't reinvent authentication or subscriptions; use proven infrastructure like Supabase and Stripe.
- Target a 4-to-8 Week MVP Launch: Longer build cycles before customer interaction increase the risk of building unwanted features.

- **Page Title (SEO):** `Custom Software vs Off-the-Shelf SaaS: The Strategic Decision Guide | Zinfell Insights`

#### Key Insights & Frameworks
- When Off-the-Shelf Wins: Commodity workflows (general accounting, basic payroll) rarely provide competitive differentiation.
- When Custom Engineering is Crucial: When your workflow represents your unique value proposition or when per-seat SaaS licensing costs spiral out of control.
- The Trap of Endless Plugin Stacks: Stringing together 12 disparate SaaS apps with Zapier creates fragility and sync delays.
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis: Custom software requires upfront capital but yields zero per-seat licensing penalties as you scale.
